# Letterform craft checklist

## Skeleton and proportion

- Establish cap height and baseline first.
- Make repeated letters instances of one master geometry.
- Compare internal white area as carefully as black mass.
- Give round forms 1–3% overshoot when they otherwise appear smaller.
- Thin horizontal strokes slightly if equal mathematical weight appears heavy.

## Counters and joins

- Keep counters open at the minimum intended size.
- Avoid tiny notches where bowls meet stems.
- Open acute joins to prevent ink traps.
- Inspect every join at 800% and at 16 px.
- Use consistent radii; do not approximate circles with lumpy Béziers.

## Kerning and rhythm

- Measure pair gaps, then judge the shapes of those gaps.
- Compare `PO`, `OB`, `BB`, `BO`, and `OP` as separate whitespace forms.
- Check the word blurred, mirrored, and reduced.
- Prevent repeated letters from creating an accidental visual pause.
- Centre optically: rounded mass and open apertures can shift perceived weight.

## SVG master

- Include `viewBox`, `<title>`, and logical IDs.
- Prefer paths and primitives; avoid `<text>` and `<image>`.
- Avoid filters, masks, and clip paths in final masters.
- Expand strokes before production delivery.
- Use integer or two-decimal coordinates and few anchors.
- Keep flat colours and no more than two colours for the wordmark itself.
