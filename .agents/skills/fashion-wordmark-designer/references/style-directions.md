# Fashion wordmark directions

## Impact / streetwear

Use a broad silhouette that reads on a label at a distance. Prefer heavy stems, compact tracking, and decisive terminals. Create ownership through one custom rhythm or cut, not a borrowed container. Avoid default italic distortion, generic varsity forms, and excessive compression that closes counters.

Useful variables:

- width: 70–95% of a normal geometric skeleton;
- stem: 16–24% of cap height;
- tracking: compact but with visible pair boundaries;
- terminals: flat, angled, or softly sheared, used consistently;
- motion: modest slant or directional cuts, never both unless the word remains calm.

## Clean / commercial fashion

Use controlled proportions, disciplined spacing, and limited customisation. The identity should survive luxury packaging, e-commerce headers, collaborations, and small product marks. Distinction may come from counter proportions, a recurring notch, or one unusual junction.

Useful variables:

- width: 85–115% of a normal skeleton;
- stem: 10–18% of cap height;
- tracking: moderate and optically even;
- radii: one or two values only;
- contrast: monoline or subtle horizontal thinning.

## Playful / collectible

Use round geometry, generous counters, and an audible letter rhythm. Keep the baseline stable and novelty controlled. Playfulness should come from proportions and repetition, not faces, characters, toys, or decorative replacements.

Useful variables:

- width: 95–125% of a normal skeleton;
- stem: 14–22% of cap height;
- counters: large enough to remain open at 16 px;
- radii: soft but systematic;
- rhythm: repeated letters share exact geometry and clear intervals.

## Reference safety

Named brands are mood coordinates, not templates. Before presenting, ask:

1. Would the design still make sense if the reference brand were not named?
2. Is the skeleton measurably different in width, weight, counters, and terminals?
3. Does the design contain an idea arising from the client's name?
4. Has every signature device of the reference been excluded?

If any answer is no, redraw.
