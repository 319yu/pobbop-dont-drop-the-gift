# POBBOP wordmark concepts

Brand name: `POBBOP` (six uppercase letters; pronounced “pop-bop”). The repeated P, O and B glyphs are built from shared masters. All concepts are original vector outlines with no live text, fonts, raster images, masks or filters.

## A — Impact Cut

Compressed octagonal construction with decisive corner cuts. It takes only the broad principles of streetwear urgency and merchandise presence from the references; the skeleton, counters and rhythm are original. Best for labels, drops and bold packaging. Risk: the most assertive and least soft/friendly route.

## B — Open Loop

Controlled rounded geometry, moderate weight and open counters. It takes commercial clarity and fashion-tech restraint as broad principles, while using a wider, softer skeleton. Best all-round route for e-commerce, collaborations and packaging. Risk: restraint gives it less instant attitude than A or D.

## C — Bubble Beat

Wide, soft modules and generous counters turn the repeated letters into a collectible six-beat rhythm. It borrows only the idea of warmth and shelf presence from collectible brands. Best for toys, accessories and colourful applications. Risk: can skew younger if paired with overly playful colours or graphics.

## D — Split Rhythm

Rounded P forms contrast with faceted O counters and split B joins. The tension gives the name an editorial, ownable cadence. Best for fashion capsules and limited editions. Risk: the angled inner cuts need careful production below very small print sizes.
