# DON'T DROP THE GIFT!

## One-sentence pitch

A fashion-physics game where the player drops reusable POBBOP gift boxes to build a fragile tower across three increasingly windy stages.

## Intended experience

Tactile, tense, playful, premium.

## Core action and controls

Time each drop while the box travels horizontally. Press Space, click, or tap to release. P or Escape pauses. Three lives are available per stage.

## Start, middle, end

The title screen teaches the action in one sentence. Each stage asks for ten points, then lets the player continue until all lives are spent. Qualified players choose the next stage or end the run. Later stages shrink the platform and add visible wind corridors. The final results screen records the local best score and creates a PNG score card.

## Technology

Vite, JavaScript, Matter.js, Canvas, Web Audio API, localStorage.

## Scope

One title screen, one game scene, three stage configurations, one level-choice screen, one results screen. Four box materials, wind corridors, PERFECT streaks, mystery rewards, butterfly moment, slow-motion collapse, procedural music, sound effects, and local score sharing.

## Art and sound direction

Warm cream, matte black, lemon yellow and silver. Editorial packaging photography, restrained typography, tactile materials and generated minimal electronic audio.
